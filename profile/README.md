> *These were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so.*
> — Acts 17:11

**Berea** is a free, open-source New Testament reader. It runs entirely in the browser, with no accounts, no tracking, and no build step.

- **Five public-domain translations:** KJV, ASV, WEB, YLT, and DRA, side by side for any verse
- **Greek text:** a word-level interlinear with Strong's numbers, morphology, and lexicon definitions
- **Church Fathers:** citations from early Christian writers (c. 100–800 AD), linked to the verses they quote
